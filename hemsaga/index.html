Hemsaga

Your Home, Organized

Everything about your home in one place

Track purchases, appliances, renovations, warranties, and photos. Add entry codes and alarm details. Filter by tags and categories. One app for all the details you'd otherwise forget.

Maintenance and supplies

Set up recurring maintenance tasks with reminders so nothing gets missed. Track supplies with low-stock alerts and link them to tasks. See completion history and upcoming schedules at a glance.

Mortgages and running costs

Track mortgages with interest rates and amortization. Log utility costs for electricity, water, heating, and insurance. View monthly breakdowns and trends over time. Keep rate history for variable mortgages.

Your data is completely private

All data stays on your device and in your private iCloud account. No company servers, no third-party services, no tracking. Export or delete everything anytime. GDPR compliant.

Premium

The free version gives you full tracking for one home. Premium unlocks unlimited homes and cost analytics. Available as yearly subscription or one-time lifetime purchase. 7-day free trial included.
