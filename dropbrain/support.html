Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

All your entries are stored on your device and in your private iCloud account. We don't operate any servers.

Does DropBrain sync between devices?

Yes. Your timeline syncs automatically between all your Apple devices signed into the same iCloud account.

How does the AI writing support work?

The AI features run locally or through Apple's on-device processing. Your writing is not sent to external servers.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Go to Settings in the app to export your entries.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to your iPhone Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions > DropBrain > Cancel.
