Look Up, Silly!

Break the Scroll Habit

How It Works

Choose which apps to block. Before you can open them, complete a quick mental challenge. This simple friction creates a moment of awareness, helping you decide if you really need that app right now. It's not about restriction — it's about intention.

Five Unique Challenges

Path Recall — remember and trace a pattern. Color Tap — match colors, not words (Stroop effect). Quick Math — solve simple arithmetic. Tic-Tac-Toe — beat the AI. Micro 2048 — complete a mini puzzle. Each takes just seconds but creates the pause you need.

Powerful Features

Block any app using the Screen Time API. Set "Always Allowed" exceptions for essential apps. Pause challenges when you need a break. Schedule blocking times for focus periods. Track your challenge statistics and streaks. Works completely offline.

Why Challenges Work

Research shows that adding friction to automatic behaviors helps build awareness. Instead of mindlessly opening an app, you take a breath, engage your brain, and make a conscious choice.

Your Data Stays Yours

Zero access to your data. All settings stored in your private iCloud account. No servers, no analytics, no tracking. Your app usage data never leaves Apple's ecosystem. We cannot see what apps you block or use.

Free Forever, No Ads

Completely free with all features. No ads, no premium tier, no locked features. Optional one-time contributions support development, but all functionality works without payment. Your focus shouldn't cost extra.

Perfect For

Anyone wanting to reduce screen time. People breaking social media habits. Those who reach for their phone on autopilot. Students needing focus during study time. Anyone who wants more intentional phone use.
