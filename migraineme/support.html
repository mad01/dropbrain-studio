Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

All your migraine data is stored on your device and in your private iCloud account. We don't operate any servers and have zero access to your health information.

Can I share my data with my doctor?

Yes. You can export your migraine history as JSON or PDF from Settings and share it with your healthcare provider.

Does Migraine Me sync between devices?

Yes. Your data syncs automatically between all your Apple devices signed into the same iCloud account.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to your iPhone Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions > Migraine Me > Cancel.

What happens if I delete the app?

Your data remains in your iCloud account. If you reinstall Migraine Me, your data will sync back.
