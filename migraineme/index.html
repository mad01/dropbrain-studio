Migraine Me

Track Your Migraine Patterns

Comprehensive Migraine Recording

Record every aspect of your episodes — intensity, duration, pain location, triggers, symptoms, and medications. Track medication effectiveness and time-to-relief, plus post-episode context like dark room use, sleep, and light sensitivity.

Triggers, Symptoms & Medications

Track triggers and symptoms from predefined lists or add custom entries. Manage medications with dosage, quantity, and timing. Rate effectiveness and time-to-relief after each episode. View statistical summaries including top triggers, most common symptoms, and medication effectiveness.

Visual Timeline & Statistics

See your migraine history at a glance with calendar views. Track patterns over time with visual indicators showing frequency and severity. Share statistical summaries with your healthcare provider.

Medication Management

Track all medications with dosage, quantity, and timing. Record what works and what doesn't. Monitor effectiveness and time-to-relief after each episode. Keep a complete medication history to share with your doctor.

Your Health Data is Completely Private

Zero access to your health information. All migraine logs, medications, and symptoms are stored exclusively in your private iCloud account and on your device. No servers, no analytics, no tracking. Export your data anytime to share with your healthcare team.

Doctor-Ready Data Export

Export comprehensive health data in JSON format to share with your healthcare providers. Generate PDF reports for easy sharing. Full GDPR compliance with built-in data export and deletion tools.

Dark Mode & Accessibility

Designed with migraine sufferers in mind. Dark mode reduces eye strain during episodes. Clean, intuitive design makes tracking quick and easy even when you're not feeling well. Fully accessible with VoiceOver support.

Premium Features

Unlimited historical tracking beyond 30 days, detailed statistical displays, advanced export options, and ad-free experience. Monthly subscription — cancel anytime.
