Frequently asked questions

Is the game free?

Yes. Full gameplay is free with no ads. Optional in-app purchases are available to support development.

Where is my data stored?

Game progress stays on your device. We don't collect or transmit any personal information.

The game is crashing or not loading

Try closing and reopening the app. If the issue persists, make sure your device is running the latest iOS version and contact us.
