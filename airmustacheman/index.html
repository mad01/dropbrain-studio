Air Mustache Man

Fly, Dodge, Survive!

Simple, Addictive Gameplay

Take control of a mustachioed flying hero and navigate through an endless sky. Swipe up to fly higher, swipe down to dive lower. Avoid obstacles, collect bonuses, and survive as long as possible. Easy to learn, challenging to master.

Dynamic Challenges

Navigate through an ever-changing sky with procedurally generated levels. Dodge spikes, mines, towering pillars, dark clouds, lightning storms, and angry seagulls. Every run is unique as difficulty progressively increases.

Collect Bonuses

Grab collectibles to boost your score. Pickles and mustaches give small boosts, coins and diamonds are worth more, and special items give massive points. Watch out for negative items that can hurt your score.

Unlock Characters

Discover and unlock new characters as you play. Start with the classic Air Mustache Man and expand your roster through gameplay achievements. Each character has a unique style.

Compete for High Scores

Track your top 3 scores and challenge yourself to beat your personal best. iCloud sync keeps your scores and progress across all your devices. Play offline anytime.

Family Friendly

Designed for players of all ages (4+). No violence, no inappropriate content, no gambling mechanics. Simple controls anyone can learn. Beautiful, colorful graphics with smooth animations. Perfect for quick sessions or extended play.

Privacy First

All game progress stored locally on your device. Optional iCloud sync uses your private account — we never see your data. No analytics, no tracking, no ads. Just pure gaming fun.

Optional Support

Love the game? Support development with optional contributions. The full game is free forever — no gameplay locked behind purchases. Your support helps fund future updates and new features.
